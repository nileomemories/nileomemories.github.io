Memories

A private photo app for two people.

Memories keeps the photos a couple takes together in one shared place: an album for every month, a "memory of the day" each morning, a calendar that shows a photo from every date, notes on each photo, and a printable photo calendar for the next year.

How it works

  • Both phones sign in with the couple's own Google account. Photos, notes and settings are stored in a "Memories" folder in that account's Google Drive, and each phone keeps a copy on the phone.
  • There is no server and no other company involved: the app talks only to Google, on behalf of the signed-in account.
  • The app can only see files it created itself (Google's "drive.file" permission). It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.
  • Memories is not available in app stores. It is a personal app, installed by its two users on their own phones.

Privacy policy

Memories app · last updated 24 November 2026

Who this is for

Memories is a personal photo app used by two people with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account's email address (Google permissions "openid" and "email"), only to check that the app is signed in with the one account it is meant for. Any other account is refused and nothing is stored for it.
  • Files the app creates in your Google Drive (Google permission "drive.file"): the photos you add, their thumbnails, your notes, the daily memory, the printable-calendar design and shared settings. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in Google Drive.
  • On the phone: the photos you choose to add from your photo library, and, if you turn it on, Face ID, fingerprint or passcode to lock the app. Face ID and fingerprint data never leave the phone and are never seen by the app.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to show your photos, notes and memories in the app on your two phones, and to keep the two phones in sync.
  • It is stored in your own Google Drive and on your own phones. There is no other server, database or cloud service.
  • The sign-in key from Google is kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Data goes only between your phones and Google, over encrypted connections.

Deleting your data

  • Photos deleted in the app go to the Google Drive trash, where Google deletes them after 30 days.
  • To delete everything, delete the "Memories" folder in Google Drive and empty the trash, and uninstall the app.
  • To withdraw the app's access, remove "Memories" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Memories' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: nileomemories@gmail.com.
